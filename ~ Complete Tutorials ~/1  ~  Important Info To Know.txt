IF YOU'RE NEW TO MODDING:
Word of Advice, don't get too carried away and go crazy all at once in terms of money, levels all that, if you want to give yourself loads of money, or levels spread it out a bit, don't just button mash those features in one day. Be more careful, you can get away with a lot with Stand but my point is too still be reasonable, 
Just don't go too over the top crazy with it in a small amount of time, there is more in info on how to be safe

BUGS SUGGESTIONS:
After you been playing for around 3 1/2 ish hours without a session restart, game restart, or maybe pc restart. Then bugs tend to start happening to you or maybe other players, 
this mainly only happens because over that 2 1/2 ish hours you've likely pressed so many buttons and spawned so many things by then. 
So do a session, game, or pc restart once these things happen
