IF YOU'RE NEW TO MODDING:
Word of Advice, don't get too carried away and go crazy all at once in terms of money, levels all that, if you want to give yourself loads of money, or levels spread it out a bit, don't just button mash those features in one day. Be more careful, you can get away with a lot with Stand but the point is too still be reasonable.
There is more in info posted on how to be safe in the CoaLwiX Discord Server.

BUGS SUGGESTIONS:
If your session becomes buggy for you or others, it's likely you have just spawned/done so much by then, or other modders are screwing with the lobby.
So do a session or game restart once these things happen.

CONSTRUCTOR POSSIBLE BUG INFO:
If you've spawned many items with Constructor at once, espiecally heavy duty ones you may run into bugs at some point. 
It's very helpful to use the Delete All Loaded Constructs button, and defiently the `cleanup` command after deleting all for a more thorough removal.
