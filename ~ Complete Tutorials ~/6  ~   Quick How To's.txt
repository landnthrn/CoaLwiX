HOW TO RIG CASINO :
In the Stand Menu go to Online > Quick Progress > Casino > Check Always Win Slot Box Machines On, > Check Blackjack On, > and Set the Number to 1 

Hit a Slot Machine twice Max Bet and get off Slot Machine after two spins (No slot machines can't be rigged for other players, just yourself)
Don't bet anymore until 48 Real Time Minutes have gone by or more (2 Los Santos Days) I would suggest not doing this more than 3 times in one day

If some players don't have VIP and can't bet then invite them into your Securo Serv Organiztion, 

if your organization is full then ask someone else with VIP to invite those that don't have it so they can bet

*NOTE*  Rigging Podium Vehicle only works for you not other players

ROULETTE :

CASINO BUG SUGGESTIONS :
If you run into bugs while rigging casino for people it's likely due to their being too many players in casino, I find bugs tend to happen when it 
gets up to 12 - 14 players in Casino at once. 

Recommend doing rigs for people in Invite-Only sessions as there tends to be less bugs.

HOW TO SAVE VEHICLES :
Go outside Los Santo Customs 

Spawn the car you want to save (Some higher end cars can't be saved, Arena War Cars can't be saved)

Go in the Stand Menu Online > Enchancements > Disable LSC Restrictions 

Drive the car into Los Santos Customs 

Purchase the Insurance for the car and drive it out, and the vehicle is now yours 

You can park it in any garage you want

HOW TO ADD F1 or BENNY'S WHEELS:
**This won't work on some cars"
(cars like the tailgater, ruiner, and more cars that can be found on the street)

1.  Spawn any car u can store in your garage
Note: If u want to Tune a car that u already own please spawn the car first as clone and delete the Car from your Personal Vehicle via Stand
Vehicle > Personal Vehicle > [vehiclename] > Delete
2.  Tune the car the way u want it with stand
Put any Benny's Wheels on (category 7 or 8)
3.  If u want F1 tires use Category 9 and front wheel 0 - 3
4.  Enable Disable LSC Restrictions
(If you are using the "Forge Model" option, turn it off as it can interfere with this method.)
5.  Drive into Benny's Shop or LSC
6.  Buy Full Coverage + Tracker
7.  Then buy any Tire Design u want (only works for Benny's Wheels not F1)
8.  Store it anywhere u want or just get out of the car and spawn the next one if u want to get more cars

HOW TO GIVE YOURSELF LEVELS :
In the Stand Menu go to Players > Select *your player name* > Friendly > Give Collectibles > All 

Press All as many times as you want to level yourself up, (No this doesn't work on other players currently got patched, just yourself)

If a title screen comes up after a lot of Collectibles just spam enter, dont worry about it

Recomend not going too crazy all in sitting, stick to maybe 300 levels a day 

I find you only really need 120+ levels anyway because thats when you unlock all cars unlocks

LOBBY CHAT COMMANDS :
Yep that's right, everyone in the lobby can do commands! 
(chat commands are enabled for everyone already in the CoaLwiX Profiles, plus the settings are set so players can't do aggressive commands like kick people) 

For other players to use the commands:

They have to type '  !  ' Followed by the correct spelling of any vehicle name and send it in game chat, or teamchat, and it will spawn for them. 

Examples  ~  !scram    ~    !bati    ~    !drifttampa  ~    !oppressor2

In the Stand Menu > Stand > Lua Scripts > Advanced Chat > Saved Messages > CoaLwiX has a pre typed instruction Message you can send players easily

If they set a waypoint somewhere and send !tpwp in chat they'll be teleported there
