**IMPORTANT NOTE**

You may find that with the Stand Menu layout its easy to accidentally hit Save instead of Load when switching between Profiles which saves new set settings to the profile ruining its intended use,
so keep in mind that there's a BACKUP of Profiles in the z Other z Folder of this package, incase that happens. 

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

HOW TO INSTALL:

Everyone has their own preferences so there are different alterations for all the profiles for you to choose from, once you picked your preference (can always change it later)

Open Stand Launchpad and click Open Stand Folder

Drag or Copy the contents from inside the type of profiles you want to use, into the Stand Profiles Folder
