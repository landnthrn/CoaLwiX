HOW TO ADD COALWIX THEME TO CHERAX :
Open you're Cherax Menu Folder

Put the coalwix folder inside your Cherax Theme Folder

Put the Startup.lua inside your Cherax Lua Scripts Folder and replace it for the one that's in there

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If you're a coder and have your own Startup.lua customizations then as you would know, add the specifics from the Startup.lua to your own
That way you don't lose your own customizations
