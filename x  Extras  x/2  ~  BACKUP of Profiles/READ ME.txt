The purpose of keeping this backup of the Profiles is because it's too easy to accidentally hit Save instead of Load in the menu and only way to fix it is by replacing the Profile with the backup version.

HOW TO INSTALL PROFILES:

Everyone has their own preferences so there are different alterations for all the profiles for you to choose from, once you picked your preference (can always change it later)

Open Stand Launchpad and click Open Stand Folder

Drag or Copy the contents from inside the type of profiles you want to use, into the Stand Profiles Folder
