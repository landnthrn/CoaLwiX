HOW TO INSTALL 100% COMPLETED STORY MODE 
 
*You don't even need mods for this, Rockstar doesn't give a fuck if you mod storymode*
 
Open Documents > Rockstar Games > GTA V or GTA V Enhanced > Profiles 

Each Folder you see with random letters and numbers represents an account.
(if you have multiple try checking the dates to figure out what ones are what)

Drag the SGTA50015 & SGTA50015.bak into the account(s) folder you want

When you launch GTA 5 you will get a prompt saying to select a save, select the one that says " June 8 2023 12:41:37 "

Press Review and then Confirm, game will launch
